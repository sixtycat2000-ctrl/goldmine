ANSWER:
The chunker works in token space: for each window of ~budget tokens it scans backward from the window's end down to at least half the budget for the best cut point, preferring boundaries in tier order — deepest paragraph boundary, then sentence end (CJK-aware), then whitespace [2]. Cut preference is measured in real tokens via the tokenizer when available; if tokenization is unavailable it falls back to the v1 char-window walk, converting the budget to characters with a kind-aware density (default 4.0 chars/token) [2]. For code, whole lines are accumulated to the same token budget, and a single over-budget line is hard-cut at a token boundary [1].
