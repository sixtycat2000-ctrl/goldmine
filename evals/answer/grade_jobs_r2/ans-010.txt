QUESTION: what do lookup queries change in the fusion weights and what kind of query counts as a lookup

(no extra note for this question)

CHECKS:
1. Normal fusion weights are meaning 1.0 and words 2.0; lookup queries invert them to meaning 0.5 and words 4.0
2. A query counts as a lookup when ANY term contains a digit and is alphanumeric-mixed or at least 4 digits long (codes/IDs)
3. The weights act on one fused score: w_m*max(z, 0) + w_c*coverage^1.5 + kind_hint*1.0 (flat, never stacks)

ANSWER:
Lookup queries invert the fusion weights `(w_m, w_c)` from the default `(1.0, 2.0)` to `(0.5, 4.0)`, halving the semantic (z-score) weight and doubling the coverage weight to favor lexical matching [2][3]. A query counts as a lookup when ANY of its terms is digit-bearing and alphanumeric-mixed, or has at least 4 digits [2][3]. This v2.1 "any term" rule replaced an earlier every-term rule, which never fired on mixed code+word queries like `LH-2209` tokenized to `lh 2209` [3].
