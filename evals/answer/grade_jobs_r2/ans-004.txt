QUESTION: how many members must a topic cluster have before it can become a distill card

A topic cluster needs at least 3 members by default: singleton and small clusters below `--min-members` (default 3) are dropped from cards [3]. The threshold is configurable via the `--min-members` flag (or config override), and clusters that fall below it are not silently discarded — the size histogram including dropped buckets is reported in `--preview` and stats, with the 10 largest dropped clusters' top terms printed so users can decide to lower the minimum [1][3].
